These platforms extend my research on educational decision-making and talent development into tools that students and families can use.

<hr class="product-divider">

<strong class="product-name">Math Wonder Island (数学奇妙岛)</strong> — AI math tutoring platform for primary-school students (Grades 1–6)
{: .product-heading}

*Lead developer & research designer · 2026–present · Currently in closed beta*
{: .product-meta}

A personalized AI math learning platform for primary-school students in China. Outstanding Project, 2026 “Chunhui Cup” (Ministry of Education of China).

<hr class="product-divider">

<a class="product-name" href="https://gaokao.yusoong.com"><strong>Yulai Gaokao AI (雨来高考 AI)</strong></a> — College-application advising platform
{: .product-heading}

*Database architecture & matching algorithm · 2025–present*
{: .product-meta}

[gaokao.yusoong.com](https://gaokao.yusoong.com)

A public-interest, AI-powered college-application decision assistant built by Yu Soong Lab (Dr. Xiaoyang Ye). It helps students navigate the gaokao application process: understanding admission rules, exploring their interests and majors, refining their application lists, and getting personalized AI guidance.

Related research: [Thinking Partner or Lookup Tool? How Students Use a Generative AI Assistant in China’s High-Stakes College Applications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) (with Xiaoyang Ye), work in progress.
