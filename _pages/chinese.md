Here you can find some Chinese-language pieces related to my research interests, including articles I've contributed to and others that feature or discuss my work. All of them are published on "依然糅春天," [Dr. Xiaoyang Ye](https://xiaoyangye.github.io/)'s WeChat Official Account. If you are interested in the economics of education, causal inference, machine learning, or Chinese college application guidance resources, I highly recommend visiting the account for more insightful materials. 😊

- [Causal Inference Checklist 番外篇：教育经济学2022年科举论文29篇](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/mYvJw6Ttz8IWcjt6Wh3yCw)  
  In this article, I helped compile a collection of job-market papers (papers #8–29 in the article) in the field of economics of education for the 2022–23 academic year, based on information shared by Joshua Goodman's Twitter. I also translated their abstracts into Chinese.
- [大学数学太痛苦了，为什么我们文科生还要学？](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/NY9J0iKLtGGmXMl7HdlYkQ)  
  This article discusses why we should study mathematics and computer science even after entering college, especially for students in the humanities. I drafted it and compiled several learning resources for mathematics and computer science (including Python, R, and machine learning), and Dr. Xiaoyang Ye refined the final version.
- [野生审稿人#2 &#124; 橡胶降价了，西双版纳的小和尚们，都在努力准备高考](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/idPmW2sesoQ6uzh9RE1Emw)  
  This article is fully credited to Dr. Xiaoyang Ye. It provides a detailed introduction and insightful commentary on my joint work with Jingcheng Jiang, "International Agricultural Price Shocks and Child Labor: The Role of Crop Choice in Rural China," which was accepted for presentation at the Young Scholars Forum on Education Finance Research in China (Peking University, 2025).
- [Yu Soong Lab的第一篇经济学论文发表了：《朋友能帮你上大学吗？》](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/i95dFYm5E7M72W6r6CsIlQ)  
  This article introduces my joint work with Dr. Xiaoyang Ye, "Peer Effects on College Choice: Evidence from Affirmative Action in China," published in Labour Economics.

Yulai Gaokao AI ([gaokao.yusoong.com](https://gaokao.yusoong.com)) — a public-interest, AI-powered college-application decision assistant built by Yu Soong Lab (Dr. Xiaoyang Ye). It helps Chinese students navigate the college-application process: understanding admission rules, exploring their interests and majors, refining their application lists, and getting personalized AI guidance. I am the main contributor to its matching database.
